\section{Mission overview (Rules V1.3)}
One fragile egg per sortie is delivered to the landing pad whose ArUco marker
(\texttt{DICT\_4X4\_50}, ids 1--6, 400\,mm on a $1{\times}1$\,m white pad)
matches the committee-assigned id, at the IAAI KMITL field. Each sortie:
take-off at the Launch \& Recovery (L\&R) site $\rightarrow$ mandatory transit
$P_1{\to}P_2{\to}P_3$ at 20\,m AGL (scored per point, both directions)
$\rightarrow$ visual search of the search area (10--20\,m band)
$\rightarrow$ \textbf{land ON the assigned pad} $\rightarrow$ release the egg
\textbf{after touchdown} $\rightarrow$ egress transit $\rightarrow$ land at
L\&R and disarm for resupply. Up to \textbf{4 sorties inside a 20-minute
window}; pad positions are unknown at take-off (blind visual search).

\textbf{Robustness for the real field.} The registry confirms a pad after 2
decoded sightings; the terminal land gate independently re-verifies the
assigned id \emph{at the pad} before committing the landing, so a
mis-registration defers (climbs, returns with the egg) --- it can never
mis-land. A pad decoded only once is topped up by a short $\sim$30--60\,s
decode visit instead of a $\sim$90--116\,s re-sweep (structural fix, validated
in I5b). Release is touchdown-gated: the egg is never dropped airborne.

\subsection{Per-sortie sequence and altitude discipline}
\begin{enumerate}[label=\arabic*., leftmargin=1.6em]
\item \textbf{GO gate} (operator enters the committee-assigned id; window clock
      starts at the first GO) $\rightarrow$ arm, take-off to 17.5\,m.
\item \textbf{Ingress transit} $P_1{\to}P_2{\to}P_3$ commanded at 19.5\,m
      (0.5\,m bias headroom under the strict 20\,m; two-stage climb closes the
      last 2\,m en route at 1\,m/s --- prevents ceiling overshoot).
\item \textbf{Search} (only if the assigned pad is not yet registered): full
      boustrophedon sweep at 12\,m; every decoded pad enters the cross-sortie
      registry; a pad decoded once gets a top-up decode visit at 10.5\,m.
\item \textbf{Serve}: direct goto at 12\,m $\rightarrow$ vision-locked descent
      rungs $12\to8\to5\to3\to2\to1.5$\,m with tightening tolerances
      (1.5$\to$0.2\,m) and descent caps ($3\to0.4$\,m/s) --- descent below the
      10\,m floor happens only here, over the pad (rules carve-out).
\item \textbf{Id-verified LAND gate}: the assigned marker must have been decoded
      during this approach, else climb and defer (never land unverified)
      $\rightarrow$ PX4 LAND at 0.3\,m/s, stay armed.
\item \textbf{Release after touchdown} (2\,s settle; airborne release is
      refused) $\rightarrow$ climb out.
\item \textbf{Egress transit} $P_3{\to}P_2{\to}P_1$ $\rightarrow$ explicit goto
      L\&R $\rightarrow$ land, \textbf{disarm} for resupply.
\end{enumerate}

\section{Verification gates before flight (do not skip)}
\begin{enumerate}[label=G\arabic*., leftmargin=2em]
\setcounter{enumi}{4}
\item \textbf{Bench (props off):} motor map; egg-servo release/hold on AUX\,9;
      V2814 in-box thrust table check (6S+1045 $\ge$1.8\,kg, $\le$25\,A);
      thrust-stand hover point ($\approx$650\,g/motor) current + temperature;
      \textbf{DXF pack health gate} (\S\ref{sec:battery}); ARK Flow CAN
      enumeration + \texttt{SENS\_FLOW\_ROT}; printed-pad decode; endurance
      bench $\ge$18\,min at 360--380\,W with 20\% remaining; System-ID re-tune
      at the real 2.6\,kg (SITL gains do not transfer; re-measure the climb
      overshoot against the 20\,m ceiling).
\item \textbf{Tethered:} stable hover, flow-lock A/B at 2\,m, camera
      calibration over a printed pad at known AGL, bench release.
\item \textbf{Free flight:} full multi-sortie mission at the practice field;
      collect real decode statistics (the registry threshold and top-up hedge
      are already sized for marginal decode).
\item \textbf{Dress rehearsal:} live eggs, full 20-min window, resupply drill.
\end{enumerate}
