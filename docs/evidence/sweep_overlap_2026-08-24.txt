overlap_frac 0.30 vs 0.44 — SITL head to head at KMITL OPTICS
=============================================================
2026-08-24. Run because the operator held overlap at 0.44 out of doubt that the
camera would find the pads at the real field, and asked for the question to be
settled in Gazebo with numbers before anything changed.

SETUP
  world      kmutnb_skyfield (there is no KMITL SITL world)
  envelope   AAVC_PROFILE=competition — transit 20 m, ceiling 20, floor 10
  sweep      12.0 m, so the 400 mm marker is 28.2 px — the KMITL condition,
             NOT the practice field's easier 8 m / 42 px
  pads       6, seed 1301, identical layout in both runs
  assigned   ids 6,3,5,1
  camera     mount_yaw 180 in config AND in the gz model (see below)
  only difference between the two runs: search.overlap_frac

RESULT
                        overlap 0.30      overlap 0.44
  sweep legs                     5               6
  pads found                   6/6             6/6
  ids correct                  6/6             6/6
  eggs delivered               4/4             4/4
  release error        0.09-0.22 m     0.15-0.20 m
  transit in order             6/6             6/6
  landed from L&R            2.0 m           2.3 m
  mission time                536 s           647 s
  verify_flight        13 ok / 1       13 ok / 1
                       (the 1 is a sampling artifact: a transit segment shorter
                        than the 1 Hz TELEM cadence, "100% of samples in the
                        corridor" on the same line)

  0.30 is 111 s faster on this field with identical coverage and identical
  delivery success. On the KMITL polygon (226 x 73 m) the computed saving is
  158 s: 5 legs / 407 s against 7 legs / 565 s.

WHY 0.44 WAS THERE, AND WHY IT NO LONGER NEEDS TO BE
  0.44 insured against an UNMEASURED camera mount. If the wide (1280 px) image
  axis did not lie across track, the real swath was the 720 px axis and 0.30
  left a 2.5 m gap per strip — a 1 m pad hides in that with 1.5 m to spare.
  The mount was MEASURED on 2026-08-23 (180 deg, four placements round the
  airframe) and search_pattern.py now derives a nose-first heading, so the wide
  axis lies across track by construction. The failure 0.44 protected against
  cannot occur.

  Separately measured the same day: the detector reads a 28 px marker at EVERY
  across-track offset out to 8.5 m of the 9.08 m half-swath (94 %). The wider
  0.30 spacing puts a worst-case pad 6.35 m off track, well inside that. So
  spacing does not fail by pushing pads somewhere the detector cannot read.

A BUG THE TEST FOUND (fixed before the numbers above were taken)
  The first 0.30 run delivered 0 of 4 with all six pads decoded and every id
  correct — positions 3.7 to 20.2 m off, the aircraft flying to empty grass at
  each pad. Cause: 2026-08-23 set mount_yaw_deg 180 in both configs (correct
  for the real aircraft) while the gz camera model was still at yaw 0, so SITL
  projections came out point-mirrored. sitl/models/eft_x6100/model.sdf camera
  pose is now `0 0 0.10 0 1.5708 3.14159` and sim matches the airframe.
  Worth keeping: this is the first time the G7-attempt-1 signature — decodes
  fine, never re-acquires — has been reproduced anywhere, and it took a mount
  error to do it. CLAUDE.md's standing warning that "SITL cannot catch a mount
  error" is now out of date: the model and the config no longer share the
  assumption.
