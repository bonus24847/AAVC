Why the takeoff gate failed a takeoff PX4 had completed correctly
=================================================================
2026-08-24, from the FC's own SD card. Two ULogs kept beside this file:
  07_22_22.ulg   59 s
  07_44_22.ulg  112 s

SYMPTOM
  `arm_and_takeoff(6.5)` sometimes raised "takeoff did not reach 6.5 m AGL
  within timeout" and aborted the whole mission at takeoff, while the aircraft
  was flying perfectly well in a stable hover.

WHAT THE LOGS SHOW
  MIS_TAKEOFF_ALT on the board ......... 6.5     (so our value DID arrive)
  NAV_MC_ALT_RAD on the board .......... 0.8     (never pinned by us)
  NAV_TAKEOFF command param7 ........... nan     (MAVSDK sends the altitude as
                                                  a parameter, not in the
                                                  command — this is normal)

  PX4 leaves AUTO_TAKEOFF as soon as it is within NAV_MC_ALT_RAD of the
  target, so it levels off at 6.5 - 0.8 = 5.70 m and never climbs further.
  Measured, mode transition AUTO_TAKEOFF -> AUTO_LOITER:

      07_22_22.ulg   t=14.1 s   5.72 m above home
      07_44_22.ulg   t=12.2 s   5.61 m above home

  Both are 6.5 - 0.8 to the centimetre. PX4 did exactly what it was told.

THE BUG WAS OURS
  `_wait_until_altitude_reached` used tolerance = min(1.0, max(0.4, 0.15*target)).
  At 6.5 m that is 0.975 m, so the gate passed only at >= 5.525 m while PX4
  delivers 5.70 m. The entire working margin was 17 cm, and it was not chosen —
  0.15*6.5 simply happens to exceed 0.8. Two real flights straddled it:

      5.70 m  -> passed by 17 cm
      5.43 m  -> FAILED by 10 cm, and the mission aborted at takeoff

  It would have been just as thin at KMITL: at a 20 m climb the percentage is
  clamped to 1.0 m, leaving 20 cm.

FIX
  The tolerance is now derived from PX4's own radius:
      tolerance = NAV_MC_ALT_RAD + 0.4
  which is a fixed 0.40 m of margin at every climb altitude instead of 17-20 cm
  of arithmetic coincidence. NAV_MC_ALT_RAD is now PINNED in both field configs
  (it never was), so a PX4 default change cannot move the gate underneath us.
  The horizontal twin has always been written this way — mission.py's
  _ARRIVAL_RADIUS_M is NAV_ACC_RAD + 1.

  Still a real check: at 6.5 m it accepts 5.30 m and rejects anything lower, so
  a thrust-loss / EKF-drift takeoff that barely leaves the ground still fails.

FOUND ALONGSIDE, NOT FIXED (operator decision, touches a failsafe)
  GF_MAX_VER_DIST = 20 m is the FC's own vertical fence and GF_ACTION = 3
  (Return). At KMITL transit is commanded at 19.5 m, so the margin to that
  fence is 0.5 m. On the practice field the same fence sits 11.5 m above the
  8.5 m transit, which is why this has never fired. Recorded maxima on this
  airframe: 19.75 m against a 19.5 m command, and a documented transient poke
  to ~20.5 m during egress — which is through the fence.
