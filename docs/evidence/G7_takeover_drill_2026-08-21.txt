PART B — D2: disarm while in a flying phase  [the NEW detector]
---------------------------------------------------------------
Real disarmed telemetry, phase forced to a flying phase. Two phases tried:

    phase=transit_ingress -> terminal=pilot_takeover after 1.00s
        AUDIT: t=1.0s disarm_in_flight_phase
        AUDIT: t=1.0s PILOT TAKEOVER (disarmed in transit_ingress) — orchestrator standing down

    phase=search          -> terminal=pilot_takeover after 0.10s
        AUDIT: t=0.0s disarm_in_flight_phase
        AUDIT: t=0.0s PILOT TAKEOVER (disarmed in search) — orchestrator standing down

(The 1.00 s in the first run is the watchdog's own 0.5 s tick cadence plus
the harness poll, not a debounce — D2 has none. The 0.10 s run shows it
firing on the first tick.)

This is the detector the field incidents needed: the pilot's disarm lands
0.46-0.48 s after the POSCTL flip (ULog-measured), under D1's 1.0 s debounce,
and the OLD armed-gate ordering then blinded the watchdog permanently.
