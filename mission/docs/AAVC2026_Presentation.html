Autonomous PX4 Quadcopter for
Precision Fragile-Cargo Delivery

Autonomous Aerial Vehicle Challenge (AAVC) 2026 · Rules V1.3 · IAAI, KMITL
Team AeroOptix · KMUTNB, Faculty of Engineering

AAVC 2026: deliver a fragile egg onto the ArUco-assigned pad, then repeat for up to four pads inside 20 minutes

  • Target — a 1 m pad with a 400 mm ArUco marker (ids 1–6); the committee assigns one id per sortie.
  • Cargo — one raw egg in a heart box; points only for an INTACT egg placed ON the pad.
  • Flight — mandatory transit P1→P2→P3 at a strict 20 m; search 10–20 m; below 10 m only over the pad.
  • Window — ≤ 4 sorties in 20 min, a per-minute penalty after; speed and reliability both score.

Three constraints drive the entire design: no RTK, no network, and an intact-egg-only score

No RTK GPS → vision owns the final metre. Coarse GPS wanders ~0.5–1.5 m, larger than the 1 m pad, so the camera must place the landing.

No internet / 4G in flight (network use is a DQ) → fully offline, deterministic. Classical computer vision — no cloud, no machine learning in the flight loop.

Intact-egg-only score → never release airborne. A broken egg scores zero, so release is hard-gated behind a confirmed touchdown.

Our answer: one deterministic quadcopter — classical vision, land-ON-and-release, fast but safe

  • Single quad-X — simplest airframe that hovers over a 1 m pad and lands vertically; no transition logic.
  • Pixhawk 6X (PX4) + Raspberry Pi CM4 — open-source flight stack (rules-compliant); deterministic autonomy on the companion.
  • ArUco decode + white-pad cue — the target is a fiducial, so a standard OpenCV decode is exact, CPU-cheap, and auditable.
  • Land-ON, touchdown-gated release — descend in verified rungs, land on the pad, release only after touchdown confirms.

A Pixhawk 6X flies; a CM4 companion sees and decides; the ground station watches and can KILL

Air segment
  • Pixhawk 6X (PX4): control, geofence, FC failsafes
  • Raspberry Pi CM4: mission, vision, watchdog
  • OV9281 nadir 1280×720 mono GS — decode + sole control
  • Gimbal pitch servo — stabilized straight-down
  • Optical flow + rangefinder — no-RTK lock
  • Egg-release servo (AUX 9)
Ground segment (GCS)
  • Primary terminal + Svelte dashboard
  • Live map, camera, mission queue + GO, KILL
  • Safety-pilot RC (dual-band ELRS)
  • Comm tower + backup power
  • ≥ 1 external display for evaluation
  • Telemetry ≥ 500 m LOS (920/2400/5800 MHz)

Every sortie flies the same verified sequence: transit → search-if-needed → land-ON → release → egress

  • PREFLIGHT ids queued once (4 of 6); confirm the egg → one-click GO
  • TAKEOFF → TRANSIT P1→P2→P3 at a strict 20 m (each coordinate scored)
  • SEARCH only if the pad is not yet known — sweep the polygon, decode every pad
  • LOCALIZE → LAND descend in rungs onto the correct pad (0.2 m final tolerance)
  • DROP release the egg AFTER touchdown confirms; skip if airborne
  • EGRESS → LAND transit home, land at L&R, disarm for resupply → next sortie

We sweep once and remember every pad — so sorties 2–4 fly direct, buying window margin

The committee reveals pads one at a time, but a sweep sees them all.

Sortie 1 flies a boustrophedon sweep of the search polygon and decodes EVERY pad into a marker-id-keyed registry (k-vote confirmed to reject a mis-decode).

Sorties 2–4 fly DIRECT to their registered pad — no sweep.

This is the key window optimisation: it turns 3 of the 4 sorties into short direct flights, so four full deliveries fit comfortably inside 20 minutes.

The egg is committed only after a decoded-id land-ON — never on a wrong pad, never airborne

Rung descent with a tightening lock. 12→8→5→3→2→1.5 m, re-centring on the decoded marker to 0.2 m before LAND.

Id-verified, centred LAND gate. The vehicle lands only with the ASSIGNED id decoded and the final rung centred — otherwise it climbs, defers, and re-approaches; the egg is never committed.

Touchdown-gated release. After PX4 confirms touchdown, a 2 s settle precedes a gentle servo release; if telemetry reads airborne, the release is SKIPPED and audited.

Propulsion is sized with margin — hover well below saturation, endurance covering four sorties plus reserve

  • Airframe: Holybro X500 V2, 500 mm quad-X
  • Motor / prop: SunnySky V2814 KV700 ×4 · APC 10×4.5MR
  • Battery: 6S 7500 mAh LiPo (166 Wh) · All-up weight (with egg): ≈ 2.6 kg
  • Thrust-to-weight: ≈ 3 : 1 · hover ≈ 33% of max thrust (~350 W / 16 A)
  • Endurance: ≈ 18 min derated (~20% reserve over the ~17-min mission)
Sizing: eCalc / momentum-theory pass (pre-bench). Climb cap MPC_Z_VEL_MAX_UP = 2 m/s keeps the climb-out under the 20 m ceiling.

Two independent failsafe layers plus a ground KILL — the vehicle recovers even if the companion dies

Companion watchdog (2 Hz)
  • Geofence / no-fly → RTH
  • Ceiling + search-floor guards
  • GPS / datalink loss → RTH
  • Battery 30% RTH / 20% LAND
  • Camera-freeze guard: reject stale frames
FC-level (fires if companion dies)
  • NAV_DLL_ACT / NAV_RCL_ACT = Return
  • GF_ACTION = RTL, RTL_RETURN_ALT = 20 m
  • BAT_* thresholds below the companion
  • All pinned + read back at startup
GCS KILL cuts motors instantly; the safety pilot can take over at any time.

In full SITL missions the system delivers 4 / 4 eggs onto the correct pad, within 0.10–0.30 m of truth, in 16.7 min

PX4 SITL + Gazebo, IAAI KMITL geometry + modelled monsoon wind; independent verifier tools/verify_flight.py (fails closed). Final flight config: single OV9281 mono nadir + System-ID-tuned gains.

The design meets the DCOS airworthiness criteria: margins, redundancy, fail-safe modes, secure retention

Performance & power margin — hover at ~33% thrust; battery for 4 sorties + reserve.

Envelope protection — PX4 tilt / yaw-rate / speed limits; ceiling-safe climb cap.

Redundancy — FC + companion split; flow + rangefinder augment GPS; GCS backup power.

Fail-safe, activatable any time — watchdog + FC RTL + ground KILL.

Secure retention — payload held in flight, released only on touchdown; PPE per role.

Conclusions

1. Determinism wins a fiducial mission. Classical ArUco decode + a verified rung descent land-ON the correct pad — no ML, no network.

2. Never spend the egg on a bad landing. The id-verified LAND gate + touchdown-gated release protect the only-an-intact-egg-scores rule.

3. Sweep once, remember, fly direct. The cross-sortie registry fits four deliveries in ~17 min; two failsafe layers + a KILL keep it safe.
